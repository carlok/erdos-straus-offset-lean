# Prompt: Target Scout

```text
We want a small mathematical research target near:

[OPEN PROBLEM, PAPER, THEOREM, OR AREA]

Find five targets that are substantially cheaper than the headline problem.
Do not merely list famous subproblems.

For each target provide:

1. a precise statement or question;
2. why it is connected to the larger problem;
3. whether the likely outcome is a counterexample, theorem, family, or
   obstruction;
4. the exact certificate that would settle one instance;
5. the smallest meaningful dimension, modulus, degree, or parameter range;
6. obvious symmetries and normalisations;
7. known results that may subsume it;
8. the role, if any, for exact computation;
9. the role, if any, for Lean;
10. a research-ROI score from 1 to 10.

Use current primary literature where possible. Distinguish:

- established theorem;
- open question;
- newly proposed strengthening;
- folklore-looking statement;
- our own exploratory question.

Reject targets whose only plausible certificate is a huge opaque computation.
Prefer targets where a successful object can be printed and checked directly.

Conclude with:

- BEST SAFE BET;
- BEST COUNTEREXAMPLE BET;
- HIGHEST-UPSIDE LOTTERY TICKET;
- CHEAPEST 30-MINUTE EXPERIMENT.
```
